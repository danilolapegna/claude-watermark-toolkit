Why this

Full manifesto

Ideas shouldn't carry watermarks.

Rewrite Room changes nothing by itself. It builds a prompt and checks surface differences. The target is wording, never thought. A statistical trace cannot tell who formed an idea or took responsibility for it.

01Provenance must not become a witch-hunt verdict.

A detector may flag statistical influence. Turning that flag into proof of authorship, fraud or intellectual laziness is judgment outsourced to a score.

02Enforcement punishes the people writing tools help most.

Dyslexia, dysgraphia, motor limitations and second-language writing can separate the quality of an idea from the ease of typing it. Treating detectable assistance as cheating measures surface production, not intellectual contribution.

03Mark synthetic evidence. Not ideas.

An image can pretend to be a camera record. Text is a symbolic carrier: a false claim is harmful because of the claim, not because software helped phrase it.

“Couldn’t I just ask another model to paraphrase it?” You could. That often means lazy synonym swaps, lost nuance and long copied sequences. This prompt forces two things at once: fidelity to your meaning and independence from the old token sequence.

What the prompt does

It masks exact values as PV markers, asks the model to rebuild from a fact ledger and keeps those markers unchanged. When you paste the draft back, this page restores the original values character for character before checking anything.
