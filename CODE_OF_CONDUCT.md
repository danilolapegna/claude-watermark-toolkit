# Code of conduct

Be precise, patient and respectful.

This project touches authorship, disability, education and institutional enforcement. Disagreement is welcome. Dismissive claims about a person's ability, language, diagnosis or motives are not.

Do not publish another person's private text, detector result, account data or correspondence. Use small synthetic examples when reporting bugs.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that are abusive, deceptive, unsafe or unsupported by evidence. Serious concerns can be sent to hello@danilolapegna.com.
