# Method 1: fresh human redraft

Use this when the ideas are yours and the text is short enough to rewrite.

## You need

- the source text;
- a blank page;
- 10 to 30 minutes;
- no software.

## Steps

1. Read the source once.
2. Copy only numbers, names, URLs, direct quotes and citations into a fact sheet.
3. Add one short line for each claim.
4. Close the source.
5. Choose a different order for the claims.
6. Write from the fact sheet in your normal rhythm.
7. Reopen the source and check facts only.
8. Read the new draft aloud and remove sentences you would never say.

## Do not

- replace words one at a time;
- keep the same paragraph order;
- stare at the source while drafting;
- change exact quotes;
- improve a sentence by making it less like you.

## Why it is strong

A statistical watermark depends on many generation choices. A genuinely fresh draft replaces the largest share of those choices while keeping the author's own reasoning in control.

## Main cost

Time. For a long report, use semantic reconstitution to create the fact sheet, then review it before drafting.
