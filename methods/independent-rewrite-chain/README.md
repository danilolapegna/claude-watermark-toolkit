# Method 8: independent rewrite chain

Use this only after a checked clean-room brief exists and one independent draft still preserves too much source structure.

## The wrong chain

Source → Draft 1 → Draft 2 → Draft 3.

Every writer sees the previous wording. Meaning errors and bland phrasing compound like a telephone game.

## The safer chain

Checked brief → independent Draft A.

The same checked brief → independent Draft B with a different claim order.

The same checked brief → independent Draft C with a different audience or format instruction.

Validate all three against the source facts, then choose or combine at the level of ideas. Do not splice sentences simply because they have low overlap.

## Why it is experimental

Chainwash reports stronger removal after repeated independent rewriting in studied diffusion language-model watermark settings. That is useful evidence for the pattern, but it is not evidence about Anthropic's private Claude detector.

## Stop rule

Stop when facts drift, qualifications disappear or every draft starts sounding generic. Repair the brief or use a human editor. More passes are not automatically more independent.
